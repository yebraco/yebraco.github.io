Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yebra Engine, the production tool operated by Yebra Co. ("we", "us"), handles information when it connects to YouTube.

Who uses Yebra Engine

Yebra Engine is an internal tool. It is used only by Yebra Co. to manage its own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from viewers.

YouTube API Services

Yebra Engine uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

  • Upload permission (youtube.upload): to upload our own videos with their title, description, tags and scheduled publish time.
  • Read-only permission (youtube.readonly): to read our channels' basic information and public statistics (subscriber count, views, likes and comments counts) of our own videos.

We do not access, collect or store personal information about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube users.

How information is stored

Authorization tokens and statistics are stored only on the computer that runs Yebra Engine, operated by Yebra Co. They are not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and are not used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept to track the performance of our own channels. Stored tokens and statistics can be deleted at any time by removing them from the computer that runs the tool. Access can also be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
