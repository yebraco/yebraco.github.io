Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yebra Engine, the production tool operated by Yebra Co. ("we", "us"), handles information when it connects to YouTube. Yebra Co. is an independent creator project run by Hugo Arbey Castrillón Buitrago, Bello, Antioquia, Colombia.

Who uses Yebra Engine

Yebra Engine is an internal desktop tool. It is used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels (the Yebra Co. channels and InfoFlashK). It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else, it has no external users, and it does not collect data from viewers.

YouTube API Services

Yebra Engine uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

  • Upload permission (youtube.upload): to upload our own videos with their title, description, tags and scheduled publish time.
  • Read-only permission (youtube.readonly): to read our channels' basic information and public statistics (subscriber count, views, likes and comments counts) of our own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only permission (yt-analytics.readonly): to read aggregated, anonymous reports about our own channels and videos (for example, average percentage viewed, audience retention, subscribers gained, shares, and the share of views by age range, gender, country, device and traffic source). These reports never identify individual viewers.

We do not access, collect or store personal information about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube users.

How information is used and stored

The information is used only to publish our own videos at their scheduled time and to understand which of our videos and topics perform better, so we can improve our content. Authorization tokens and statistics are stored only on the computer that runs Yebra Engine. They are not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, are not combined with data from other sources, and are not used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are refreshed daily from the YouTube API while the authorization is active and are kept only to track the performance of our own channels. Stored tokens and statistics can be deleted at any time by removing them from the computer that runs the tool. Access can also be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page; if access is revoked, the related stored data is deleted within 30 days.

Content and AI

Our videos are created with the help of AI tools (script writing, voice and editing). Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is published. When a video contains realistic synthetic content, it is disclosed as such on YouTube.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
